Privacy Policy

The short version

Quick Spend is an independently developed Android expense-tracking app. You can record expenses without creating a Quick Spend account or connecting Google. The app stores its financial records on your phone. Google Sheets sync and file exports are optional.

This policy explains the Android app and this information website. Contact the Quick Spend developer at mohamedmunif99@gmail.com with questions or privacy requests.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the amounts, transaction types, categories, notes, dates, budgets, recurring schedules, templates, currency choices, and local profile details you enter. A profile photo is optional and is selected using Android's photo picker.

Expense databases are encrypted. Settings and connection details are stored using encrypted preferences backed by Android Keystore. If you set an app PIN, the app stores a verification hash rather than the plaintext PIN. These measures reduce risk; they cannot guarantee protection on every device or against every threat.

Profiles separate app records and settings on one phone; they are not separate Android system accounts. PIN protection is optional. Hide amounts is a display preference, not an authentication lock.

Google account and Google Sheets data

If you choose to connect Google Sheets, Google handles account selection and authorization. Quick Spend does not receive your Google password.

  • Account identity: the app requests openid and userinfo.email and retrieves your email address to associate the correct Google account with the connection.
  • File access: the drive.file permission is used for the spreadsheet created by Quick Spend or files you explicitly open with the app. It is not a request to read every file in your Google Drive.
  • Spreadsheet content: new synced expenses include date, amount, category, note, transaction type, currency, and a stable Sync ID used to prevent duplicate retry writes.
  • Local connection metadata: the Google email address, spreadsheet ID, connection state, sync status, and pending upload snapshots are stored on your device. Access tokens are used in memory and are not saved in app preferences.

The app communicates directly with Google APIs over HTTPS. The current implementation does not send your spreadsheet or Google access tokens to a Quick Spend-operated backend.

Google user data is used to provide the requested account connection and spreadsheet sync. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. We follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Human access is not part of ordinary syncing; if you voluntarily send information for support, it is used to address that request.

Your sync choices

You can use the app without Google Sheets. Connecting enables uploads of newly saved expenses. Existing history, edits to an expense, and deletions are not automatically reflected in Google Sheets. Pending records preserve the original values at save time, including currency.

Disconnect from Settings → Google Sheets to stop this profile's syncing. The app attempts to revoke Google access unless another local profile still uses the same connection account. You can also remove access through your Google account's third-party connections.

Disconnecting does not delete your spreadsheet, local expenses, or saved connection reference. To remove an uploaded row or spreadsheet, delete it in Google Sheets or Drive. A request already in progress may finish even if an expense is deleted locally.

Other services and information sharing

Currency conversion: the app sends the selected currency codes to Frankfurter to request an exchange rate. The conversion amount and expense records are not sent for that lookup. As with normal network requests, the service receives connection information such as your IP address.

Backups: when you export a backup, it is saved to a location you select through Android. If you choose a cloud storage provider or share the file, that provider or recipient receives it. Modern exports are password-protected; older plaintext JSON backups can still be imported. Keep your backup password separately.

Support: emailing us shares your email address and whatever you include with the developer and email service providers. Please send only what is needed. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to answer the request and address legitimate service or legal requirements.

The current Android app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Google and other services process their own service data under their policies, including Google's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

App records stay on your device until removed, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Pending sync records are retained for retry until completed or the related records are permanently removed. Individually deleted expenses may be retained temporarily to support Undo.

Settings → Security lets you delete expenses. This does not delete profile details, all settings, exported backup files, or data already uploaded to Google Sheets. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes app-local records and may remove the encryption keys needed to read them. Export and verify a backup first if you need to keep your history.

Manual expense backups contain expense history, categories, and currency information; they are not a complete backup of profiles, PINs, connection details, pending sync jobs, templates, recurring schedules, or budgets. Delete exported copies from the storage locations you chose when you no longer need them.

The developer cannot remotely restore or erase expense databases that exist only on your phone. For data you sent directly to support, contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

This website

This site provides app information and policy pages. Its own code does not include analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, account registration, or a form that collects expense information. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, and request logs to deliver and secure the site. Hosting-platform sign-in or security features may have their own notices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. Material changes to the use of Google user data will be disclosed and additional consent requested when required. This app is not designed as a service specifically for children.

For questions about this policy or your data, email mohamedmunif99@gmail.com.
