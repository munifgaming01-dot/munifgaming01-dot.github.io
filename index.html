Google Sheets sync is your choice. Local expense tracking works without connecting a Google account.

See exactly what is shared
  1. 1

    Choose your account

    Connect from Settings and grant access through Google's authorization screen.

  2. 2

    Let Quick Spend create a sheet

    The app creates its spreadsheet in your account. It requests access to files it creates or that you explicitly open with it—not all of your Drive files.

  3. 3

    Save a new expense

    New entries are queued for upload. If connectivity drops, they stay on your phone for retry.

Sync appends new entries. Existing history, later edits, and deletions are not automatically copied to Google Sheets. Disconnecting keeps your spreadsheet.
